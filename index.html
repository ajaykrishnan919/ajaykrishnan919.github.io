01

Automated sand testing laboratory

A mechatronic cell that runs the standard foundry sand tests (green compressive strength, permeability, moisture and grain fineness) with no one standing at the bench. A Doosan collaborative arm carries specimens between stations.

I designed the system and wrote the ROS 2 manipulation tasks with MoveIt Task Constructor. A path cache lets repeated transfers reuse trajectories that were already planned instead of planning them again.

Where
Sahas Labs, Maharashtra
Role
System design, motion planning
Stack
ROS 2, MoveIt 2, MTC, Doosan cobot
02

Solar panel cleaning robot

A tracked robot that drives along photovoltaic arrays and sweeps them with rotating brushes. I worked across the whole robot: I contributed to its mechanical design, designed its electronic hardware, and wrote its entire firmware stack on an STM32H563 running Azure RTOS ThreadX. The firmware is split into deterministic threads for motors, commands, telemetry, UART and battery.

  • Mechanical design of the robot, from CAD through to the built prototype shown below.
  • Electronic hardware design for the robot.
  • CANopen SDO motor-control library for IDS-C servo drives over FDCAN.
  • Firmware validated on hardware. Field trials are ongoing.
Where
Sahas Labs, Maharashtra
Role
Mechanical design, electronics hardware, embedded firmware
Stack
STM32H563, ThreadX, CANopen / FDCAN, I²C, UART
03

EpiSHOT

A reusable epinephrine autoinjector

Single-use autoinjectors are expensive, and people with severe allergies need to carry them everywhere. EpiSHOT is a reusable alternative with three levels of safety, a needle that retracts on its own after the dose, and an ergonomic grip. We built it, then cycled it more than 500 times to test the mechanism.

It won the James Dyson Award 2022 as the national winner for India.

Where
BEES Lab, IISc Bangalore
Role
Mechanism design, prototyping

04

EEG headband for hearing screening

Hearing tests on newborns depend on recording tiny evoked potentials, and those recordings are only as good as the electrode contact. I designed a modular headband that adjusts to head size and holds the electrodes against the skin steadily enough to extract auditory brainstem responses (ABR) and mismatch negativity (MMN).

  • Published in Biomedical Signal Processing and Control, 2024.
  • 1st runner-up at the Siemens SmartX Health Hackathon 2022 and at SHIFT 2022 (Siemens Healthineers).
Where
BEES Lab, IISc Bangalore
Role
Headband design, fabrication

Other research at IISc

Continuum surgical robot
An MRI-compatible, tendon-driven continuum robot for outlining brain tumours. Its flexure joints are 3D printed and generated parametrically for each application. Tip position was checked with optical encoder feedback under stepper actuation. ICRA 2023 · IROS 2023 · BSN 2023
Diffuse reflectance spectroscopy probe
A handheld probe with LEDs at several wavelengths, built to tell tumour from healthy breast tissue during surgery. Its biocompatible enclosure is SLA printed. Scientific Reports 2025 · Patent IN442474-001
Fluorescence imaging system
Optical design for a handheld imager with paired NIR and visible cameras, used to watch blood flow during surgery with ICG dye. I worked out the field of view, working distance and filter specifications.
